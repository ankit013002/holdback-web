    How the estimate works

    Self-employed people pay self-employment tax (Social Security + Medicare, ~15.3% of net earnings) on top of federal income tax, and often state income tax. Holdback adds these up for your situation and divides by your income to get the share of each payment to hold back. It also applies the standard deduction and the QBI (§199A) deduction where they apply.

    Common questions

    What percentage should a 1099 worker save for taxes?
    For many self-employed people it lands around 20–30% of net profit, but it depends on your total income, filing status, and state. Use the calculator above for your number rather than a rule of thumb.
    Do I have to pay quarterly estimated taxes?
    If you expect to owe $1,000 or more, the IRS generally expects quarterly estimated payments (around Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, and Jan 15). Holdback builds the four payments and reminds you before each deadline.
    Is this tax advice?
    No. Every figure here is an estimate based on what you enter and published 2026 tax figures. Confirm with a qualified tax professional.
    Holdback is a tax-planning estimate tool, not a tax advisor, accountant, or filing service, and does not connect to your bank. Estimates only — not tax advice.
