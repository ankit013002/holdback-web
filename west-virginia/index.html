    How much should a self-employed West Virginia resident set aside?

    Every self-employed person pays self-employment tax (Social Security + Medicare, ~15.3% of net earnings) plus federal income tax and West Virginia income tax. The calculator above adds these for your income, filing status, and applies the standard and QBI deductions where they apply.

    Common questions

    Does West Virginia tax self-employment income?
    Yes — West Virginia taxes income, so a self-employed resident owes West Virginia income tax in addition to federal self-employment and income tax.
    What percent should I set aside for taxes in West Virginia?
    It depends on your income and filing status. For a single filer earning $60,000 it is roughly 23%. Use the calculator above for your exact number.
